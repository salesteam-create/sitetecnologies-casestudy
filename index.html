01 / OverviewThe Problem
The Problem

Manual inventory.
Modern leasing velocity.

CenterPoint recognized that legacy inventory tracking — handwritten notes, visual estimates, and unstructured photo documentation — was slowing down workflows and creating inconsistencies across a growing portfolio.

These legacy processes, while once effective, no longer met the speed and accuracy demands of today's leasing environment. The team needed a modern solution to access accurate, high-resolution property data remotely, reduce administrative burden, and support timely, trustworthy marketing materials.

02 / SolutionAerial inventory intelligence
The Solution

High-resolution

SITE's Inventory AI delivered drone imagery and AI-assisted identification of critical industrial features — letting the CenterPoint team verify counts remotely, with confidence, without waiting on a manual site visit.

A Navigate by Time layer compares any site against its own past, building greater confidence in how each property has evolved over time.
